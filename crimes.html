Common Cyber Crimes

Learn about the most prevalent cyber threats and how to protect yourself.

Phishing

Fraudulent messages trick you into sharing personal details.

High

Online Fraud

Fake shopping websites, lottery scams, calls demanding money.

High

Cyberbullying

Harassing or humiliating people through digital platforms.

Medium

Malware & Ransomware

Malicious software locks or damages your data.

High

Identity Theft

Stealing personal information to impersonate you.

High

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing confidential info.

Medium

✅ Safety Checklist

  • ✔ Use strong, unique passwords
  • ✔ Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • ✔ Keep software and apps updated
  • ✔ Install antivirus software
  • ✔ Avoid clicking suspicious links
  • ✔ Use secure connections (HTTPS)
  • ✔ Review privacy settings
  • ✔ Backup important data regularly
