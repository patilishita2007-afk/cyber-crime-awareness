❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I create a strong password?

Use at least 12 characters, include numbers, symbols, uppercase and lowercase letters.

What is phishing?

Phishing is a scam where attackers trick you into giving personal details via fake emails/messages.

Is it safe to use public Wi-Fi?

Public Wi-Fi is risky. Avoid banking/logins. Use VPN if needed.
